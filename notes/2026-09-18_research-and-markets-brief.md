## Part 1 — Paper digest: "Attention Is All You Need"

**"Attention Is All You Need"** (Vaswani et al., [arXiv:1706.03762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)) introduces the **Transformer**, a new and deliberately simple sequence-transduction architecture that is based solely on attention mechanisms and **dispenses with recurrence and convolutions entirely** — dropping the recurrent and convolutional neural networks that underpinned the dominant encoder–decoder models of the era. On two machine translation benchmarks the Transformer delivered better translation quality while being significantly more parallelizable and requiring far less time to train. Its headline result is **28.4 BLEU on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task**, improving over the then-existing best results, including ensembles, by more than 2 BLEU.

*Source: abstract of arXiv:1706.03762.*
